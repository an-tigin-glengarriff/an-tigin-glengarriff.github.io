Things to see & do

Activities.

Days out nearby.

Gardens, beaches, boat trips, stone circles and more, from Glengarriff out to Beara, Kerry and West Cork. For hikes and island loops see our Walks page, and for food see Eat. Many places are seasonal, so check opening times before you go.

Activities & sights (click a pin for details) An Tigín

Glengarriff

  • Bamboo Park ↗
    Exotic seaside garden of bamboos, palms and tree ferns (seasonal)    Glengarriff
  • Bantry Bay Charters ↗
    Boat trips and sea angling on Bantry Bay; book ahead in summer    Glengarriff Pier
  • Pooleen Wood & waterfall ↗
    Quiet woodland pool, a lovely spot for a swim    Glengarriff

Bantry & inland

  • Bantry House & Garden ↗
    Historic house and terraced gardens above the bay (seasonal)    Bantry
  • O'Donovan Cycles ↗
    Bike hire in Bantry town    Bantry
  • Waterfall Alpaca Farm ↗
    Alpaca walks on a family farm, by appointment only    Drimoleague

Beara

  • Cashelkeelty Stone Circles ↗
    Bronze Age stone circles, a short uphill walk from the car park    Lauragh
  • Uragh Stone Circle ↗
    Stone circle in a dramatic lake-and-mountain setting    Tuosist
  • The Naked Sheep: Alpaca Trekking ↗
    Guided treks with alpacas    Tuosist
  • Lios Lara Riding Stables ↗
    Horse riding on the Beara Peninsula    Castletownbere
  • Dzogchen Beara ↗
    Buddhist retreat centre on the cliffs; meditation sessions (book online)    near Allihies
  • Ballydonegan Beach ↗
    Sandy beach below the old copper-mining village    Allihies
  • Garinish Beach ↗
    Small, sheltered beach near the tip of Beara    near Dursey Sound

Kenmare & Kerry

  • Bonane Heritage Park ↗
    Stone circle, ring fort and other ancient sites on a short trail    Bonane
  • Dromquinna Stables ↗
    Horse riding by Kenmare Bay    Kenmare
  • Derrynane House ↗
    Daniel O'Connell's ancestral home, with gardens and woodland    Caherdaniel
  • Derrynane Beach ↗
    Long sandy beach and dunes beside Derrynane House    Caherdaniel
  • Kells Bay House & Gardens ↗
    Subtropical gardens with tree ferns and a rope bridge    Kells, Ring of Kerry

Sheep's Head & Mizen

  • Ahakista Stone Circle ↗
    Small stone circle in a garden setting by the village    Ahakista
  • Barleycove Beach ↗
    Dune-backed sandy beach near Mizen Head    near Crookhaven

Baltimore & Skibbereen

  • Inish Beg Gardens & Trails ↗
    Island estate with gardens and woodland trails    near Baltimore
  • Aquaventures ↗
    Whale and dolphin watching trips, diving and snorkelling    Baltimore
  • Fastnet Lighthouse ↗
    Ireland's Teardrop, seen on a boat trip from Baltimore or Schull    off Cape Clear
