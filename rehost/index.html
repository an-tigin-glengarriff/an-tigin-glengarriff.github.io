Is your website broken?

Five ways a website quietly stops working.

Most owners only find out when a customer mentions it. Here’s what visitors actually see, and what we do about it.

What customers see

Expired certificate

Visitors get a full-screen “Your connection is not private” warning. Most turn back, and Google marks the site as not secure.

How we fix it: We install free, auto-renewing certificates and monitor them, so the padlock never lapses.
What customers see

Lapsed domain

The domain renewal was missed, and now the address shows a parked “for sale” page or nothing at all. Customers think you’ve closed.

How we fix it: We recover or move the domain into your name, set it to auto-renew and track the renewal date for you.
What customers see

WordPress critical error

An out-of-date plugin or theme crashes the site, leaving a blank page with “There has been a critical error on this website.”

How we fix it: We rebuild the site on fast, low-maintenance hosting with nothing to patch, or repair and update it.
What customers see

The host’s wrong certificate

Your hosting company serves its own certificate instead of yours, so browsers say the site might be impersonating you.

How we fix it: We set up the certificate for your own domain and check it from outside every day.
What customers see

Server errors

500 and 503 errors, timeouts and suspended hosting accounts. The site is down, and nobody notices until a customer mentions it.

How we fix it: We rehost on reliable infrastructure with 24/7 uptime checks, and we fix problems before customers see them.
