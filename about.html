About Us

A private, warm & cosy seaside cabin on Bantry Bay, nestled between the mountains and the sea.

Our Story

We're Geri and Shane, and An Tigín is our little off-grid cabin on the Wild Atlantic Way, just 3km from the picturesque village of Glengarriff in Co. Cork, an idyllic escape from the hustle and bustle of everyday life, a place to unplug and recharge your mind and body.

Whether it's a calming swim and a sauna, or simply curling up with a good book, we hope you'll find An Tigín the perfect place to unwind and connect with yourself and nature.

Living Simply, Off-Grid

We take real pride in our eco-friendly approach. An Tigín is a glamping experience like no other, so there's no mains electricity or Wi-Fi, but here's what you will find:

  • ✓4G/5G mobile coverage if you need to stay connected
  • ✓A portable power bank and LED lighting, plus a spot to charge your devices
  • ✓Hot water for washing and showering
  • ✓A composting toilet
  • ✓Simple cooking: a single ring gas cooker
  • ✓Pure well water, drawn from our own well bored 360 feet into the rock, naturally free from fluoride and chemicals
  • ✓A sauna and the sea for a calming swim

Please note we don't provide kayaks, but there are local guides if you'd like to book a trip with them.

Getting Here & Around

Our spot strikes a lovely balance between tranquillity and convenience. Glengarriff village has shops, restaurants, hotels, a pizzeria, a pharmacy and some very inviting pubs, with bus connections to Cork City, Killarney and beyond. A leisurely boat trip on the bay is well worth it too.

Bantry is 21km away and Cork Airport is 85km. As we're 3km outside the village, we'd recommend having your own transport so you can explore at your own pace.

Good to Know

An Tigín is an adults-only retreat, so we're unable to accommodate children or pets. Thanks for your understanding.
